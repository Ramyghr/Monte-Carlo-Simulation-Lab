Portfolio VaR Methodology

Cholesky Decomposition

Generate correlated returns from independent standard normals:

Σ = L * Lᵀ (Cholesky factorization) correlated_returns = L * z (where z ~ N(0,I))

Σ is the covariance matrix, L is lower triangular matrix

Portfolio Statistics

  • 1
    Expected Return: μₚ = ∑ wᵢ μᵢ
  • 2
    Variance: σₚ² = ∑∑ wᵢ wⱼ σᵢ σⱼ ρᵢⱼ
  • 3
    Sharpe Ratio: (μₚ - r) / σₚ

Component VaR

Contribution of each asset to total portfolio VaR:

Component VaRᵢ = (∂VaR/∂wᵢ) × wᵢ

Measures marginal contribution considering correlations

Diversification & Optimization

Correlation Impact

Perfect Positive (ρ = 1.0) No diversification
Zero Correlation (ρ = 0.0) Moderate benefit
Perfect Negative (ρ = -1.0) Perfect hedging

Diversification Benefit

Portfolio VaR Reduction
Diversification = (1 - VaR_portfolio / Σ VaR_individual) × 100%
  • • Reduces portfolio risk without sacrificing return
  • • Most effective with low/negative correlations
  • • Provides "free lunch" in modern portfolio theory
  • • Critical for institutional portfolios

📖 Portfolio Risk Management Applications

🏛️

Regulatory Compliance

Basel III requires banks to calculate portfolio VaR for market risk capital. Internal models must account for correlation effects.

⚖️

Portfolio Optimization

Identify optimal asset allocations that minimize VaR for a given return target, or maximize return for a given risk tolerance.

🛡️

Risk Budgeting

Allocate risk budgets across assets based on Component VaR contributions. Ensures risk-taking aligns with investment views.
