Greeks Definitions

Delta (Δ) ∂V/∂S

Change in option price for $1 change in underlying. Range: [0,1] for calls, [-1,0] for puts.

Gamma (Γ) ∂²V/∂S²

Rate of change of Delta. Highest for at-the-money options. Important for delta hedging.

Vega (ν) ∂V/∂σ

Change in option price for 1% change in volatility. Always positive for long options.

Theta (Θ) ∂V/∂t

Time decay per day. Usually negative for long options (lose value with time).

Rho (ρ) ∂V/∂r

Change in option price for 1% change in interest rates. Less significant for short-dated options.

💡

Practical Applications

Delta Hedging

Maintain delta-neutral portfolio by holding -Δ shares of underlying per option contract.

Hedge Ratio = -Delta × Contract Size

Portfolio Risk Management

  • Delta: Directional exposure
  • Gamma: Hedging costs and rebalancing frequency
  • Vega: Volatility risk exposure
  • Theta: Time decay P&L

Trading Strategies

Long Gamma: Straddles, Strangles
Short Gamma: Iron Condors, Calendars
Long Vega: Vol plays before events

Finite Difference Method

Central difference approximation:

Δ ≈ [V(S+ε) - V(S-ε)] / 2ε

ε = 1% of spot is typical. Too small → numerical errors. Too large → approximation errors.
