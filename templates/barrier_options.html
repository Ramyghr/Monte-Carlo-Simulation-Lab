Barrier Option Pricing

Price barrier options that activate (knock-in) or cease to exist (knock-out) when the underlying asset price hits a predetermined barrier level. Barrier options are path-dependent and typically cheaper than vanilla options, making them popular for cost-effective hedging strategies in range-bound markets.

Knock-Out Options

Up-and-Out

Option ceases if S ≥ H. Cheaper when barrier is far from spot.

Down-and-Out

Option ceases if S ≤ H. Popular for protecting downside.

Knock-outs are cheaper than vanilla as they may expire worthless before maturity

✅

Knock-In Options

Up-and-In

Activates only if S ≥ H. Cheaper upfront cost.

Down-and-In

Activates only if S ≤ H. Used for triggered protection.

Knock-ins activate only when specific market condition occurs

💡

Key Properties

In-Out Parity

Knock-In + Knock-Out = Vanilla

Monitoring

Continuous monitoring increases knockout probability vs discrete

Rebates

Some pay rebates if knocked out, increasing value

Market Applications

💰

Cost Reduction

30-50% cheaper than vanilla options when barrier is set appropriately. Ideal for budget-conscious hedgers.

📊

Range Trading

Popular in FX markets for trading within expected ranges. Knock-outs work well in stable environments.

🎯

Structured Products

Embedded in autocallables, reverse convertibles, and other structured notes for retail investors.
