Theory

Longstaff-Schwartz Method: American option pricing uses backward induction to determine the optimal exercise policy at each time step.

V(t, S_t) = max(Φ(S_t), E[e^(-rΔt) V(t+Δt, S_{t+Δt}) | S_t])

Early Exercise Premium: The additional value of American options over European options due to the right to exercise early.

American Price = European Price + Early Exercise Premium

The algorithm uses least squares regression to estimate continuation values at each exercise date.
