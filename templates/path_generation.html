Theoretical Background

Geometric Brownian Motion

dSₜ = μSₜdt + σSₜdWₜ

Models asset prices with constant drift and volatility parameters.

Euler-Maruyama Scheme

Ŝᵢ₊₁ = Ŝᵢ + μŜᵢh + σŜᵢ√h·Zᵢ
  • • Strong order: 1/2
  • • Weak order: 1
  • • Simple implementation
  • • Universally applicable

Milstein Scheme

Ŝᵢ₊₁ = Ŝᵢ + μŜᵢh + σŜᵢ√h·Zᵢ + ½σ²Ŝᵢ(Zᵢ²-1)h
  • • Strong order: 1
  • • Weak order: 1
  • • Higher accuracy
  • • Includes Itô correction term
🎯

Convergence Properties

Strong Convergence

E[|Ŝ - S|] ≤ Chᵞ

Measures path-wise accuracy:

  • • Euler: γ = 1/2
  • • Milstein: γ = 1

Weak Convergence

|E[f(Ŝ)] - E[f(S)]| ≤ Chᵞ

Measures distribution accuracy:

  • • Important for option pricing
  • • Both schemes: γ = 1
  • • Sufficient for expectations

Exact Simulation Methods

Advanced acceptance-rejection sampling eliminates discretization bias using Brownian bridge construction.

Based on Beskos and Roberts (2005) exact simulation methodology

Reference:
