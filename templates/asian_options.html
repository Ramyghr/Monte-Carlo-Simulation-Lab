Asian Options Theory

Payoff Formulas

Call: max(AT - K, 0)
Put: max(K - AT, 0)

where AT is the average price over the period

Average Types

Arithmetic: AT = (1/n) Σ Si

Standard average, no closed form

Geometric: AT = (∏ Si)1/n

Has closed-form solution

Volatility Reduction

σavg ≈ σ / √3

Averaging reduces effective volatility by approximately √3

Features & Applications

Advantages

  • Lower cost: 20-40% cheaper than vanilla options
  • Lower volatility: Reduced by ~40-60%
  • Anti-manipulation: Harder to manipulate average
  • Smoother payoff: Less sensitive to terminal spike

Common Applications

  • Commodity trading: Average oil/gold prices
  • FX hedging: Average exchange rates
  • Energy markets: Average electricity prices
  • Employee compensation: Stock option grants

Market Practices

  • Arithmetic average more common in practice
  • Daily fixing typically used for averaging
  • Popular in Asian and commodity markets
  • Often embedded in structured products
