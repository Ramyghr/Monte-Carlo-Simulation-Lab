Asian Options Theory

Payoff Formulas:

Call: max(Aᴛ - K, 0)

Put: max(K - Aᴛ, 0)

Average Types:

  • Arithmetic: Aᴛ = (1/n) ∑ Sᵢ
  • Geometric: Aᴛ = (∏ Sᵢ)^(1/n)

Geometric average options have closed-form solutions, while arithmetic average options require numerical methods like Monte Carlo.
